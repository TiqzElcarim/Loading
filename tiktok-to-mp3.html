How to convert TikTok to MP3

  1. Open TikTok, tap Share on the video, then Copy link.
  2. Paste the link in the box above and tap Download.
  3. Tap Download Audio (MP3) to save the sound to your phone.

The MP3 is the original sound used in the video, so you can use it as a ringtone, in your edits, or just listen offline. Need the video too? Use the TikTok downloader without watermark.
